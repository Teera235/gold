FIELD READINESS: NOT CLEARED FOR EXCAVATION

Previous T01-T06 rankings are WITHDRAWN. The previous workflow omitted a -1000 BOA radiometric offset before division by 10000. This was checked against the product XML of the first input scene; the revised workflow requires offset metadata for every input scene. Recalculated observations do not confirm gold.

Evidence gaps
- No independent Au assay, verified mineral occurrence or site geological assessment supports these coordinates.
- Sentinel-2 ratios are broad surface proxies. Vegetation masks are not validated land-use classes.
- Images span 2023-2025 and cannot certify present site conditions.
- The 95th percentile necessarily selects relative highs; weights are uncalibrated.
- Two exposed observations do not prove persistent bare soil across all dates.
- Copernicus GLO-30 is a surface model resampled onto a 30 m UTM grid, not a surveyed ground terrain model.
- Access rights, excavation permission, UXO clearance, slope stability and underground conditions have not been verified.

Recommended visit scope
1. Meet the site owner and a qualified local geologist. Verify site access and current permissions with local authorities.
2. Have local qualified personnel confirm UXO assessment/clearance before any disturbance. This map cannot establish clearance.
3. From established safe access, record GNSS coordinates, timestamped photographs, exposed rock/soil, vegetation, roads, workings and drainage. Do not treat any old T-ID as a digging instruction.
4. Only after safety and permissions are established, have the geologist design representative and control sampling with an appropriate laboratory, including blanks, duplicates, certified reference materials and chain of custody.
5. Decide further work from geological observations and independent laboratory results. Neither a bright pixel nor a revised R-ID is a gold finding.
